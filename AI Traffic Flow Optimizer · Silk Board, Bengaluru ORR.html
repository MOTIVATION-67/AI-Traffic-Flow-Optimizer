How the AI decides (Slide 5)

COUNT: a camera or road sensor counts the vehicles waiting on each road (here, the queue length stands in for that count). DECIDE: green time = (road’s queue ÷ all queues) × green budget, never below the minimum or above the maximum. Example: queues 40, 20, 10, 10 and budget 160 s → 80 (capped at 70), 40, 20, 20 s. If a road empties after its minimum green, the signal ends early. SWITCH: every green goes to yellow (3 s) then all-red (2 s) before the next road. If the sensors stop, the smart signal falls back to the fixed timer. An ambulance request ends the current green safely and gives that road 10 s of green.

Limits of this model: one signal only (no linked signals along the ORR); no turning movements, pedestrians, buses stopping or lane changes; the Silk Board flyovers that now carry some traffic over the junction are not modelled, only the ground-level signal; arrivals are random but identical for both signals.
