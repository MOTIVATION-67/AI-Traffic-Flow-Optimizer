How it works

Count: the smart signal reads the queue on each of the four roads (standing in for a camera or road sensor).
Decide: green time is proportional to each road’s share of the total queue, kept between a minimum and maximum limit. A green ends early if the road empties.
Switch: every green is followed by yellow (3 s) and all-red (2 s). If sensors fail, the signal falls back to the fixed timer. An ambulance request safely interrupts the cycle and gives that road a short green.

Features

Side-by-side fixed vs smart signals fed the same random traffic
Mixed Indian traffic (two-wheelers, cars, autos, buses) with capacity measured in passenger-car units, and left-hand driving
Morning peak, evening peak and off-peak scenarios, with editable road names, vehicle counts and lanes
Results table and chart for vehicles cleared per hour, average wait and longest queue, plus estimated time and fuel saved

Limitations

Traffic data is simulated, not counted on the road
Models one signal only, with no turning movements, pedestrians or lane changes
Silk Board’s flyovers are not modelled, only the ground-level signal
